WEATHER ICONSAnimated in the simple style of a phone weather app. Each loops here; in the game it plays once when the weather changes, then holds its last frame.
RAINThe sun slides out as a cloud slides in, then rain falls.
STORMThe cloud covers the sun, then a lightning bolt flashes.
SNOWThe cloud covers the sun, then snowflakes drift down.
HEATWAVEThe sun swells while its rays shrink and spin faster.
CLEARWhen weather ends: the sun on its own, rays turning slowly.
