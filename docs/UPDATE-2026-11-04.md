## 2. Phase 3 follow-ups (build now)

### 2a. Line cost badges
Badges show "−1" with the stat's icon (Energy, Appetite or Morale). Paved shows "FREE". The tooltip spells it out, e.g. "Steep: −1 extra Energy to cross".

### 2b. Simple and Informative modes
Add a UI setting in Settings (☰ menu) with two modes:
- **Simple** is the default.
- **Informative** is the current screen.

Remember the choice in `localStorage`, wrapped in try/catch. It's one global setting, not one per window.

**The goal of Simple mode is fewer words, not fewer visuals.** Slots, icons, bars and the inventory grids all stay.

| Panel | Simple mode |
| --- | --- |
| Party | One compact card per hiker: portrait, name, a tiny family icon instead of the word, 3 small injury pips by the name, and the three status bars with the number at the end of each bar. No separate stats list, threshold text, READY/ACTING tags or expand arrows. The selected hiker glows in their party color, and hikers who have acted are faded. Clicking a hiker opens a pop-out with full details (exact stats and thresholds, conditions, trait slots, XP) that closes when you click away. |
| Biome card | "MEADOW · 1/3" only. |
| Day bar | "DAY 2" plus three phase icons with the current one highlighted, and no words. Weather is an icon; clicking it shows the description and forecast. |
| Objective | A small pill under the day bar (e.g. "GOAL 9/14") that expands when clicked. |
| Abundance | Show caps only. A node's Abundance appears only when it's low, as a small warning on that node's icon. |
| Turn panel | Only the actions the selected hiker can take right now, as larger buttons with no cost line (the cost shows in the move or scavenge preview). Equipped, Bag and Resources stay as the normal slot grids, with short labels and counts ("BAG 3/4"). No helper sentences, and no Abilities section when there are none. |
| Map | Unchanged, except the checkpoint label shows only on hover. |
| Trail log | Starts minimized. |

**Informative mode** is the current screen, plus small tick marks on the status bars at each threshold.

## 3. Phase 4 (scavenging): decisions to plan with

Follow `docs/mockups/Scavenge.png`, `ScavengeMods.png` and `ScavTypes.png`, plus Phase 4 in the build plan. Decided:

**Rarity odds** (in `balance.ts`):

| Luck | Common | Uncommon | Rare | Very rare | Legendary | Mythic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 75% | 15% | 6% | 2.5% | 1% | 0.5% |
| +5 (high) | 50% | 25% | 14% | 7% | 3% | 1% |

- Interpolate per point of Luck between the two rows, and clamp.
- Negative Luck pushes Common higher, up to about 85%.

**Other decisions**
- **Mixed card on a 3-node tile:** 1 resource from each of 2 random nodes on that tile.
- **Dogs:** get 1 extra card to choose from when scavenging. This replaces the old "more Choice cards".
- **The window** (already in the plan): light and quick.
  - A translucent, blurred panel.
  - No "how it plays" strip.
  - Bonuses behind a "N bonuses" pill.
  - Cards dealt face down and clicked to flip, with a "flip all" option.
  - Cards built from a blank backdrop, the rotating rays, and the icon or glyph.

## 4. Plan changes for later phases (update BUILD_PLAN.md; don't build yet)

### Phase 8: Checkpoints and ranger stations
(Now Phase 9: see `UPDATE-2026-11-07-items.md`.)
The station is a full screen in a cozy wood-and-parchment style. Follow `Station.png`, `StationAhead.png` and `Station.dc.html`.

**The room**
- The back wall is a full-width window wall looking into a darkened forest: layers 241, 271, 266, 265 at roughly brightness 0.42–0.6, with wooden frames and a faint glass glare.
- On the left is a stone fireplace with a mantel. The flames flicker in layers, and warm firelight pulses gently across the room (off under reduced motion).
- A hanging wooden sign shows the station theme.
- The shopkeeper stands behind the counter (placeholder art until Wyatt's arrives).

**Style**
- Wooden pieces: the sign, counter, tabs, price tags and buttons.
- Dark translucent panels from the main game UI: the shopkeeper dialogue, the sell slot and the hiker packs.

**Top-right buttons**
- **Rest & Go:** the free night, then the next biome.
- **Preview Next Trail** (binoculars icon): opens the next section's map, showing only what's visible from here. Specials that make choices about the next trail are used from this preview, e.g. Cement Mixer (choose a line to pave) or scouting services (choose tiles to reveal).

**Shopkeeper dialogue:** a dark speech bubble in Edit Undo, with three options.
- **"What's selling?"** shows this visit's market as chips with resource icons and flat cap changes (green +X, red −X). The station theme sets which resources pay more or less. Roll the amounts each visit, with the ranges in `balance.ts`. Selling uses these prices.
- **"What's ahead?"** describes the next biome (tier, weather odds, common nodes, likely conditions and line types) with icon chips. Placeholder text until the biomes are finalized.
- **"Just looking"** closes the conversation.

**Tabs:** Shop and Workbench. Blueprints are sold in the Shop tab.

**The counter (Shop tab)**
- **Row 1:**
  - a "Drag here to sell" slot (dark panel; it shows the price before confirming);
  - item cards, built from the item card layers;
  - station specials mixed in with the items, as cards with a unique animated gold border.
  - Every card has a parchment price tag that dims when the party can't afford it.
- **Row 2:** blueprints as larger blueprint-paper squares with a border in their item type's color, then resource bundles.
- **Scrolling:** rows 1 and 2 scroll sideways together with one scroll bar along the bottom of the counter, in dark wood rather than gold. Drag, mouse wheel or swipe. The last visible card fades at the edge.
- **Party caps:** a brass plaque fixed at the right end of row 2. It doesn't scroll. Caps stay one shared purse.

**Below the counter**
- Every hiker's pack (bag and resources), shared while at the station, with room for bigger slots.
- Drag from a pack to the sell slot to sell, or to the Workbench to craft.
- **Hovering** a card in a hiker's pack (long-press on phone) shows a larger version of the card with its sell price at this station.
