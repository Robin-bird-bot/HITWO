## 1. Rule changes

Update `docs/design/` and `balance.ts`, and fix any tests that assumed the old values.

| Rule | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep | +2 Energy, or +3 in a shelter | **+1 Energy, or +2 in a shelter.** A night without sleep still costs 2 Energy. |
| Tend | No cost (day only) | **Costs 1 Energy.** Still day only. |
| Morale at 0 | The hiker takes an injury | **No injury.** Morale stops at 0, and the hiker can't do anything that costs Morale. |
| Morale and Luck | High Morale gives more Luck, low gives less | **Morale sets a base Luck: −2 at Morale 0, 0 at Morale 5, +2 at Morale 10,** in even steps between (0.4 per point). Traits and items add on top. This feeds the rarity odds table from the Nov 4 update. |

Suggested `balance.ts` names: `SLEEP_ENERGY`, `SLEEP_SHELTER_ENERGY`, `TEND_ENERGY_COST`, `MORALE_LUCK_MIN`, `MORALE_LUCK_MAX`.

**Conditions** (add to `docs/design/02-traits-and-conditions.md`, Negative conditions):
- **Frozen:** can't move or be moved.
- **Bleeding:** when it runs out, the hiker gains 1 injury. If it's cured before then, there's no injury. Cured by specific tools, traits and clothes (Wyatt will name them).
- **Wet** (addition): if a Wet hiker would get Wet again, they become **Soaked** instead, for whichever is longer: the Wet time they had left or the new time.

## 2. UI changes (build now)

### 2a. Movable windows
Windows can be dragged around like real computer windows.
- **Which:** every pop-up (scavenge, challenge, inventory, hiker details, settings) and the floating panels (party, turn panel, trail log). The map itself stays put.
- **How:** drag by the window's title bar or header, with a grab cursor there. Windows stay inside the 1600×1000 stage and snap to its edges when dragged close.
- **Pop-ups that block the game** (like challenges) still block it, but can be dragged aside to see the map behind them.
- **Remember** each window's position in `localStorage` (wrapped in try/catch). Add "Reset window layout" to Settings. Double-clicking a title bar puts that window back in its default spot.
- **Phone (later):** dragging can be turned off there if it gets in the way.

### 2b. Actions blocked by a status are red, not grey
- When an action can't be taken **because of something abnormal** (a condition like Frozen, an injury, Morale at 0, Exhausted, and so on), show it in **red** (`#E5624A` tint and border) instead of grey. It can't be clicked. Hovering or tapping it says why, e.g. "Frozen: can't move for 1 more phase".
- **Simple mode:** these red actions **stay visible** instead of being hidden, so it's clear something is wrong. Actions that are unavailable for normal reasons (Sleep during the day, Tend at night, nowhere to move) are still hidden in Simple mode and grey in Informative mode.
- **Informative mode:** red instead of grey for the same cases.
- Build this as a general "blocked by" check that conditions can plug into, since most conditions arrive in Phase 6.

### 2c. Threshold ticks in Simple mode too
The tick marks at each threshold on the status bars now show in **both** modes:
- Energy: ticks at 6 (Fatigued) and 3 (Exhausted).
- Appetite: ticks at 5 (Hungry) and 2 (Famished).

## 3. Phase 5: challenges, opportunities and Trials

Read `docs/design/04-challenges.md` first, especially "How to read a challenge". This section adds to Phase 5 in `docs/BUILD_PLAN.md`; update that phase to match.

### 3a. Three kinds of pop-up
| Kind | Color | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Challenge** | Red `#C8403A` | Pay a cost or take a penalty. Party challenges ("If not:") are paid together; each-hiker challenges ("If failed:") are judged per hiker. |
| **Opportunity** | Blue `#2F6FAE` | A reward. None are written yet; keep a few placeholder ones (`placeholder: true`) so the flow works. |
| **Trial** | Orange (pick a warm orange that reads on the dark UI, around `#E0812F`) | Do something before a deadline instead of paying. |

**Risk challenges** are a red challenge variant marked "Risk:" in the Cost column (Dry Mouth, Thin Ice, Blazing Sun, Dangerous Current, Storm, Lightning, Insane Wind, Bear With a Gun!!, Hypothermia). They have no pass or fail. Each hiker may spend resources or items to lower a chance or a loss, then the game rolls.

### 3b. Content as data
- Put every challenge and Trial from `04-challenges.md` in `src/content/challenges.ts` as typed data: id, name, kind, tier, nodes, gates, scope, art, description, cost options, penalty effects, and X/Y ranges.
- **Descriptions** are placeholders for now (`description: '…', placeholder: true`). Wyatt is writing them.
- **Gates** from the Nodes column:
  - `[snow only]`: only in the snowy, high part of the trail (Phase 11 biomes). Until biomes exist, add a debug toggle that counts the section as snowy.
  - `[stormy weather only]`, `[sunny weather only]`: weather (Phase 6).
  - `[afternoon only]`, `[only if a Dog is on the tile]`.
  - A gate that isn't built yet makes the challenge unavailable, never broken.
- **Effects** are small typed operations run by one effect engine: lose or spend a resource, item, caps, a stat, durability, XP or tool uses; gain a condition or an injury; change Abundance; move hikers (back, swept, blown); change lines (Muddy, Rough); hide nodes; raise an unrevealed tile's tier; and so on. No `if (challenge === 'Flies')` checks.
- **Effects that need a later phase** (most conditions' real effects, crafting, sleep, eating, weather, durability) still apply in data form. For example, a condition is added to the hiker with its duration and icon. Mark the missing behavior `TODO(phase N)` and list them in your summary. **Frozen** is simple, so make it block moving now.

### 3c. Rolling X and Y
- **X** is rolled once per challenge when it's revealed. The [bracket] range in the doc is the Tier I range. When the same challenge appears one tier higher (a Challenge special space), scale the range with a factor in `balance.ts` (`TIER_SCALE`, `TODO(wyatt)`). Some ranges run backwards: for Dangerous Current, a lower X is harder.
- **Luck** shifts X in the party's favor using the average Luck of the hikers entering the tile.
- **Y** is rolled separately for each hiker, using that hiker's own Luck.
- **(25%) before a penalty** means that penalty is included only with that chance, rolled when the challenge is revealed. Show it only if it was included.
- Text in **[brackets]** is game logic and never shown. Text in **(parentheses)** is shown on the card.
- Durations: "phases" for short effects, "days" or "nights" for longer conditions. One turn = one phase.

### 3d. Picking a challenge
- When a hiker enters an unrevealed tile, pick from the challenges whose nodes match a node on that tile, whose tier matches the section's tier, and whose gates pass. Use weights in `balance.ts`, and avoid repeating a challenge within the same section.
- Mix in Trials and opportunities with odds in `balance.ts` (`TODO(wyatt)`).
- A tile's challenge triggers once. Moving onto an unrevealed tile by any means (moving back, being swept or blown) triggers its challenge.
- **Only the hikers on that tile** are affected unless the text says otherwise.

### 3e. The pop-up (follow the mockups)
- Red or blue blurred background with the drifting Game Quotes `!` pattern; the tall banner with the title centered, type and tier on the left, scope on the right.
- Art at 16:9 (`object-fit: cover`), from `public/assets/challenges/<id>.jpg`, or a placeholder.
- The quote and description, then the "If not" or "If failed" box (or "Reward" for opportunities).
- Per-hiker sections with the requirement squares, "OR" between alternatives, a status line, and ✓/✗.
- The inventories of the hikers on the tile along the bottom, with items and resources on separate rows.
- **Risk challenges:** each hiker's section has a +/− for each resource or item they can spend, and a live readout of their chance or loss ("Chance to fall in: 24%"). A "Roll" button replaces ✓/✗.
- **Trials:** the same window in orange. The banner shows the deadline ("2 phases left") where the scope would go, and the goal and penalty replace the cost. There's a "Got it" button.
- The window is movable (2a).

### 3f. Trials
- After the pop-up closes, an **orange countdown chip** stays on screen near the day bar, showing the Trial's name and the time left. Clicking it reopens the details.
- Any target tiles, rows or lines are **highlighted on the map** (Rocco's tile in red).
- The goal is checked at the end of every phase. Meeting it early ends the Trial. Missing the deadline applies the penalty.
- **Rocco Raccoon's Trials** give rewards that stack (within 1 day you get all three; within 2 days, the resources and caps; within 3 days, the caps). Finishing on day 4, or reaching a checkpoint first, gives no reward and no penalty. Not done after 4 days gives a random negative condition.
- At most 2 Trials active at once (`balance.ts`).
- Build the Trial framework and every Trial whose goal is about position or time now. Trials that check sleeping, eating, crafting or Sitting by the Fire use the same framework but stay off until their system exists (`TODO(phase N)`).
