## 3. Wonders (new)
A Wonder is a famous landmark with a big group goal and a big reward. Read the Wonders section of `04-challenges.md` and follow `Wonder.dc.html` and `WonderRewards.dc.html`.

### 3a. The tile
- Trail generation places **exactly 1 Wonder tile in every biome**, at a random spot.
- The tile has **no nodes**, so nobody can scavenge there.
- Until it's revealed, it looks like any other unrevealed tile.
- It's revealed when a hiker moves onto it, or when its challenge is revealed another way (Old Firewatch Tower, Multnomah Falls' reward, a Warning Bird, and so on).

### 3b. When it's revealed
- Pick 3 of that Wonder's 5 goals at random, and roll their X values.
- **X scales by biome:** the ranges in the doc are for biome 1, and each later biome multiplies them by `WONDER_BIOME_SCALE` in `balance.ts` (`TODO(wyatt)`). Ranges marked − go down instead (Crater Lake's tool limit).
- Pick the 3 Tier III items or traits for the "Pick 1 of 3" reward now too. Use placeholders until Tier III content exists.
- The Wonder pop-up opens. Afterwards a **Wonder icon** stays on the tile. Clicking it reopens the pop-up from anywhere on the map, and so does moving back onto the tile.

### 3c. Goals
- Goals are checked live, against **only the hikers currently on the Wonder tile**. Each one ticks as soon as it's met.
- Some goals need **run counters** that don't exist yet. Add them to `GameState` now (saved, and in tests):
  - challenges passed and failed, per hiker;
  - items crafted, per hiker, by type;
  - days since each hiker last slept;
  - shelters placed, per biome.
- "Every resource type" (Multnomah Falls 5) means the 4 node types: 2 Forest, 2 River, 2 Wildlife and 2 Mountain resources.

### 3d. Accomplishing it
- The **Accomplish** button is greyed out ("1 GOAL LEFT") until all 3 goals are met at the same time.
- Pressing it lets **each hiker on the tile pick 1 of the 3 rewards**.
  - Two hikers can't take the same Tier III item or trait. A pick that's already taken shows that hiker's face on it and is greyed out for everyone else.
  - Fort Rock's "2 shelter cards" can be picked by at most 2 hikers.
- "Claim rewards" applies them. After that the Wonder is **accomplished**: its tile and icon turn grey, and its pop-up becomes read-only.
- Number placeholders in `balance.ts`, all `TODO(wyatt)`:
  - "a lot of XP" (first guess: about one level's worth);
  - "a lot of caps" (about one good shop item);
  - Pittock Mansion's caps goal.

### 3e. The pop-up
- **Bigger than the other pop-ups:** 1400×956 on the stage, and movable like the others.
- **The ribbon** across the top uses the **trinket card back's animated gradient** (the same colors and the same slow shift), with "WONDER · BIOME N" on the left, the name centered in large Edit Undo, and the number of hikers here on the right. No shift under reduced motion.
- **The art** runs across the full width of the window as a banner (`object-fit: cover`), with a dark fade at the bottom for the description (a placeholder for now).
- **Below the art:** the goals checklist on the left and the 3 rewards on the right, then the Accomplish button.
- **The background** is the same blurred, drifting `!` pattern as the challenge pop-ups, tinted purple.
