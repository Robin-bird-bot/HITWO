## Classes

A class says what a character is good at. It isn't strict: a character can have one or two classes. Classes also decide which traits a hiker is offered when leveling up (see below).

| Class | What it's about |
| --- | --- |
| Tank | Helps the group move forward. Takes costs and burdens for others, or blocks them. |
| Hardy | Keeps their own stats and conditions in check, and recovers easily. |
| Scavenger | Better scavenging, of everything or of specific resources. Draws more cards and holds more resources. |
| Crafter | Crafts higher Quality items, has better recipes, and holds more resources and items. |
| Scout | Reveals tiles, goes off alone for an advantage, and moves fast. |
| Support | Helps other hikers: cures or prevents conditions and looks after items. |
| Jack | Jack of all trades: odd or mixed traits with no clear category. |
| Entertainer | Keeps the party's Morale up: singing, art, campfire stories. |
| Moneymaker | Caps: sells for more, buys for less, finds caps, and makes the most of ranger stations. |

### Leveling up by class

- Each character has 1 or 2 classes.
- A level-up's Trail and Utility options come from the hiker's classes, from Jack traits, and from general traits that fit any class (Strong, Backtracker).
- To keep some surprise, \[1\] of the 3 options can come from any class.
- **Morale traits are open to everyone.** They aren't tied to a class, and new Morale traits made for a character can be learned by any hiker. A few existing ones stay special (Misophonia, Rain Lover, Moody, Show Girl).
- Special and unique Trail and Utility traits still only go to the characters they belong to.

## The Plush family

A fourth family, next to Human, Dog and Cat: stuffed animals that aren't quite real. More Plush characters can come later.

**Built in for every Plush hiker:**

- **Stuffing:** Energy and Appetite are combined into one bar, Stuffing, with a maximum of \[12\]. Plush hikers still eat and sleep, but every Energy or Appetite cost and gain goes to Stuffing.
  - Low Stuffing: at \[7\] or less they can't move the group and can only sleep at night; at \[3\] or less they can only sleep; at 0 they take an injury.
- **Carried:** can't move alone. Being moved with the group costs nothing and doesn't use their action. Someone has to carry them.
- Traits that check for Dogs don't count them, because they aren't real dogs.

## Variants

Variants are costumes or alternate versions of a hiker with a different focus.

- **No cloning:** a variant can't be picked if that hiker, or another of their variants, is already in the party.
- **Unlocks:** most characters, and especially variants, are unlocked later rather than available at the start. So unlockable characters can be a bit stronger, like a 4th slot.

## Holiday hikers

Christmas variants of Gdog, Leah, Ellie and Cody, built to play together. **Grammy Clause** is the heart of the group: she's the only way to make Presents, and the other three each get something special out of opening them. Like every variant, each one can't join with their base hiker (no cloning). In the Family sort they get their own **Holiday** ribbon.

**Presents** are a new item only Grammy Clause can make (see Items). Opening one costs a hiker their action and 1 Energy, and gives a random item from any tier.

Gdog in a Santa suit. She can't craft normal items, only Presents, and she can't open them herself, so she needs the party around her.

## Groups

No group traits for now. Instead, characters who belong together have traits that fit together, so they play well as a team. Achievements or challenge runs for these groups ("finish a run with the Red Pack") could come later.

**Erin, Jackson, Anni, and Boss & Gracie** (a family)

- **Anni** scouts ahead: Scout reveals the nodes around her, so the family knows where the Forest tiles are.
- **Jackson** scavenges those Forest tiles with Green Thumb and Archaeologist, and Tends them to keep Abundance up for everyone.
- **Erin** crafts on the same tile as her kids (Collaborative) and teaches them her blueprints for half the Morale (Teacher).
- **Boss & Gracie** carry supplies between them when they're spread out: Boss fetches resources in, and Gracie sends them out. Herder brings the humans along for free.

**Cody, Duke and Scarlet** (the Red Pack)

- **Cody** leads the group into challenges and takes the bad conditions and injuries himself (Alpha Dog).
- **Duke** can give Cody his action (Good Boy), so Cody can move the group twice in one phase.
- **Scarlet** eats when the others eat (Spoiled) and carries the extra item (Strong). As a Hardy dog, she's the one who stays standing.
- Their Morale traits pull them apart a little: Duke likes just one buddy, and Cody prefers Humans to other Dogs. They play best split into pairs, like real siblings.

**Grandpa Sherman, Madeline, Pearl and Kai**

- **Pearl** wanders off alone to explore (Wanderer) and scavenges alone for free (Pirate).
- **Kai** keeps up with her for free (Trailer) and keeps her Energized (Hype Dog), even from the next tile.
- **Sherman** sweeps everyone back up: Tank Butt lets his group move bring hikers who already acted, so Pearl and Kai can explore first and still come along. Once per biome, Trespasser lets him skip a day's challenges.
- **Madeline** patches everyone up once they're back together: Therapist shortens their conditions, and Comedian lifts the tile's Morale at the fire.

## Sorting the hiker selection screen

- **Default:** starters first, then the rest. No ribbons.
- **Family:** grouped by real-life family (the table below). A hiker in two families shows in both. Variants go in Other, unless the table gives them a family (the holiday hikers have their own Holiday ribbon), so they don't crowd the family groups.
- **Class:** grouped by main role. Each group's ribbon uses that role's banner color.
- **Traits:** grouped by how many traits a hiker starts with (open slots don't count).
- **Difficulty:** grouped by the bands above, easiest first.
- Inside a group, hikers go by name (by rating first when sorting by difficulty).

The sort buttons, in order: Default, Family, Class, Traits, Difficulty.
