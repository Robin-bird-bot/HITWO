# Items

Every item so far, by type, with its tier, effect, recipe and art. Tools, food and clothes have a Quality from 1 to 5 (see Quality in the main tab). Shelters and trinkets have no Quality.

- **(X a/b/c/d/e)** is a value that changes with Quality: a at Quality 1, b at 2, c at 3, d at 4, e at 5. Quality 3 is the most common, so the middle number is the normal one. Many values only change at Quality 1 and 5. Example: **(X 1/2/2/2/3)** uses.
- **Art** is Wyatt's file number. The game uses the cleaned-up copies named after each item.
- **Tier** is 1 to 3. Tier 4 only comes from Luck.
- **Look at** a challenge means the player sees it, but the tile stays unrevealed and its challenge still triggers. **Reveal** means the tile is revealed on the map.
- **\*** marks tools built for the hidden-challenges idea (see the Challenges tab). If that idea is dropped, they can be cut or changed.

## Crafting

### Basic recipes (generic craft)

Each type has **one key resource** (pick from 2) plus a **broad** part that can be any mix from a wide group. Paying it shows 2 or more random items of that type at the crafter's tier, and they pick 1.

| Type | Key | Broad | Total (Tier 1) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tool | 2 Metal or Wood | 3 Mountain or Forest resources (any mix) | 5 |
| Clothes | 2 Fur or Moss | 3 Wildlife or Forest resources (any mix) | 5 |
| Food | 2 Fish, Meat or Mushroom | 2 Forest or River resources (any mix) | 4 |
| Shelter | 3 Wood or Stone | 3 resources of any kind | 6 |
| Trinket | none | 5 different resources picked at random, 1 of each. A new 5 is picked every time a trinket is made. | 5 |

**Higher tiers** \[first guess\]: Tier 2 adds 1 key and 1 broad resource; Tier 3 adds 2 of each.

### Blueprint recipes

Every item also has its **own recipe**, used once the hiker knows its blueprint. It names exact resources that suit the item and costs more than the basic recipe: about **+1 resource at Tier 1, +2 at Tier 2 and +3 or 4 at Tier 3**. In return, the hiker gets exactly that item. "Any" in a recipe means any resource.

### Clothing durability

Counted in days, ticking down at camp, by tier: **Tier 1 (X 4/5/5/5/6), Tier 2 (X 5/7/7/7/9), Tier 3 (X 8/10/10/10/12)**. Some clothes also lose durability when their effect triggers.

- Plain food (only Appetite) gets a chance of a negative condition, usually Food Poisoning for 1 day.
