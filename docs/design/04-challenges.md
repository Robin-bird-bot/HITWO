# Challenges

Work in progress. The challenge list by tier with nodes, then ideas still being decided, other kinds of costs, and Trials. Challenges are red, opportunities blue, Trials orange.

## How to read a challenge

- **Who it affects:** only hikers on this tile, unless the card says otherwise.
- **Party or each hiker:**
  - Party challenges are shared: anyone on the tile can chip in, and the penalty line starts **"If not:"**.
  - Each-hiker challenges say **"Each hiker spends…"**. Every hiker pays for themselves, and the penalty line starts **"If failed:"**.
- **Words:**
  - **Spend** for costs (never "discard").
  - **Lose** for penalties.
  - **Across the party** when the party shares a cost.
  - **(any mix)** when one slot takes several resource types.
- **X** is rolled once for the whole challenge, within a range set by its tier. The number in \[brackets\] after X is the Tier I starting range. The average Luck of the hikers entering the tile shifts X in the party's favor.
- **Y** is rolled separately for each hiker, using that hiker's own Luck. Example: "Frozen for Y\[1–3\] phases" can be 1 phase for one hiker and 3 for another.
- **A (%) before a penalty** is the chance that penalty is included when the challenge is revealed. Example: "(25%) X\[15–30\]% chance to become Blind" is on the card one time in four.
- **\[Brackets\] in a card's text** are instructions for the game and aren't shown. Text in (parentheses) is shown on the card.
- **Durations:** short effects count **phases** (one turn = one phase), and longer conditions count days or nights. Wyatt's "turns" are written as phases below.
- **Nodes:** each challenge lists the node types it can appear on, and can only show up on a tile with one of those nodes. **Any** means any tile.

**New conditions:** **Frozen** (can't move or be moved) and **Bleeding** (when it runs out, the hiker gains 1 injury; curing it first means no injury). Both are in the Conditions list.

**Risk challenges** (Dry Mouth, Thin Ice, Storm, Lightning and others marked "Risk") have no pass or fail. The danger is a chance or a loss that starts high, and each hiker may spend resources or items to lower it before it's rolled. The pop-up needs a +/− for each resource with the odds or loss updating live.

**\[Snow only\]** marks challenges for the snowy, high part of the trail. They're mostly Tier III and can appear on most nodes. **\[Stormy weather only\]** and **\[sunny weather only\]** mark challenges that need that weather.

**Art** is the number of Wyatt's file in the challenge art folder (1920×1080). A dash means no art yet; use a placeholder.

### Balance notes

- **Ranges I changed from your list** (change back if you disagree):
  - Black Cat X\[3\] → X\[1–2\], since every hiker pays it.
  - Straight Path X\[2–5\] → X\[2–4\].
  - Adrenaline Spike X\[5–10\] → X\[5–8\].
  - Thorn Thicket X\[3–10\] → X\[3–6\].
  - Freeagle X\[5–12\] → X\[5–8\].
  - Dangerous Current X\[5–15\] → X\[8–12\]. This one runs backwards: a lower X is harder, so X drops at higher tiers.
  - Storm 10 → X\[8–10\].
  - Fashion Critic: 1 cap per 2 durability instead of per 1. At 1 per point, three fresh clothing items could cost 15+ caps, more than the party has early.
- **Lots of injury penalties:** Squirrel Ambush and Bobcat (Tier II) and Snow Leopard and Abominable Snowgirl (Tier III) all give a straight injury. If they show up too often, Squirrel Ambush could be a 50% chance instead.
- **Bleeding is a kinder injury.** It gives time to cure it (with specific tools, traits and clothes), so a longer Bleeding is easier.
- **The Eagles are a pair on purpose:** Majestic Eagle is the party version and Toupee Eagle the each-hiker version. Bear! and Bobcat are close too (Bleeding vs. a straight injury).
- **Same condition, same node:** Rock Band and Echo Canyon both give Ringing Ears on Mountain tiles. Rock Band could move to Any.
- **Morale costs matter more now.** A hiker at 0 Morale can't spend any, so Morale challenges (Strong Breeze, Echo Canyon, Adrenaline Spike) fail automatically for them.
- **Other pairs:** Flies and Gnats, Withered Forest and Low Water Levels, and Strong Breeze and Sharp Wind appear on different nodes and tiers.
- **Tourist Trap:** X stays at 1–2 early, because caps are scarce.
- **Torn Pack and Group Climb are Tier II,** because spending items and sending everyone back are harsh early.
- **Spooky Ghost!:** Nightmares only matter at night. Consider letting it appear only in the afternoon or at night.
- **Coverage:** every condition is now used (Mold Invasion and Spreading Plague use Food Poisoning). Tier I has 36 challenges, Tier II 22 and Tier III 13.
  - **Trials that overlap:** Train!! and Rockslide! have the same goal (leave the tile by the next phase), and Spreading Plague and Stampede both mean "don't share a tile." Each pair differs in deadline and penalty, but you could cut Rockslide! or move it to Tier II.

## Other things hikers could spend

New costs beyond resources, items, caps, stats and durability:

- **Tool uses:** spend a use, not the whole tool.
- **An action:** a hiker gives up their next action to help ("Hold the rope").
- **XP:** spend X XP.
- **Luck:** accept being Unlucky for a few days as the price.
- **Quality:** an item drops 1 Quality.
- **Abundance:** spend X of a node's Abundance on this tile (nature pays the cost).
- **One brave hiker:** a single hiker pays the whole party's cost, or takes 1 injury so everyone passes.
- **Leave something behind:** drop an item on this tile. Any hiker can pick it up later.
- **Skip sleep:** a hiker can't sleep tonight.
- **Time:** every hiker on this tile stays put for 1 more phase.

**Penalty ideas beyond the whiteboard:**

- **Lines:** every line connected to this tile becomes a type (Muddy, Rough).
- **This tile:** no shelters here for X days.
- **Luck:** −Luck on the surrounding nodes.
- **The map:** an unrevealed tile's challenge goes up a tier, or revealed tiles become hidden again.

## Trials (orange)

Trials ask hikers to **do** something within a deadline instead of paying.

**How they work**

- They're revealed like any challenge, in an orange pop-up that shows the goal, the deadline (X phases or days) and the penalty.
- After the pop-up closes, an orange countdown chip stays on screen. Any target tiles, rows or lines are highlighted on the map.
- The goal is checked at the end of every phase. Meeting it early ends the challenge, and some give a small reward. Missing the deadline applies the penalty.
- Unless stated, they apply to the hikers who were on the tile when it was revealed. Party goals say so.
- At most 1 or 2 can be active at once.

## Wonders (purple)

Wonders are famous landmarks with a big group goal and a big reward.

**How they work**

- **The tile:** every biome has exactly 1 Wonder tile, placed at random anywhere in the biome. It has no nodes, so nobody can scavenge there.
  - Near the start of a biome, it's hard because hikers usually move ahead to meet the goals and then backtrack.
  - Near the end, it's hard because hikers can't work toward goals they haven't seen, unless they reveal it early another way (Old Firewatch Tower, Warning Bird, and so on).
- **Revealing:** when a hiker reaches the tile, or its challenge is revealed another way, the Wonder pop-up opens.
  - The game picks 3 of the Wonder's 5 goals at random and shows them as a checklist.
  - It also picks the 3 Tier III items or traits for the "pick 1 of 3" reward at that moment.
- **After that:** the Wonder stays on its tile with a Wonder icon. Clicking the icon, or returning to the tile, reopens it.
- **Goals** are checked live, against only the hikers currently on the Wonder tile. Each one ticks when it's met.
- **Accomplish:** when all 3 goals are met at once, the Accomplish button lights up. Pressing it lets each hiker on the tile pick 1 of the Wonder's 3 rewards.
  - Two hikers can't take the same Tier III item or trait. Some other rewards have limits too (Fort Rock's shelters: at most 2 hikers).
- **Done:** an accomplished Wonder turns grey on the map and in its pop-up, and can't be done again.
- **Scaling:** the X ranges below are for the first biome. Each later biome raises them (more resources, more caps, more completed challenges). Ranges marked − (like Crater Lake's tool limit) go down instead.

**The pop-up**

**Balance notes**

- **The resource goals cover all 16 resources once.** Witch's Castle, Fort Rock, Crater Lake and Sleeping Giant each ask for one resource from every node type, and no resource repeats.
- **Some goals need more than one hiker** (Multnomah Falls 2, Swiss Alps 4, Sleeping Giant 1 and 2). That's fine, since bringing the group together is part of the point.
- **Goals that pull against each other** make good puzzles. Sleeping Giant 1 and 2 (lots of Energy combined, but everyone tired) need 2 or more hikers. None of the 5-goal lists has two goals that can never be met together.
- **Numbers still needed:** Pittock Mansion's caps, and how much "a lot of XP" and "a lot of caps" is. A first guess: XP worth about one level, and caps worth about one good shop item.
- **Crater Lake 2:** if Abundance tops out at 10, this means River Abundance is full.

**Balance notes**
