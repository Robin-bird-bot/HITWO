Hike in the Woods sound board

Every sound the game needs, where it plays, and a button to hear it. Loops can be layered (try the meadow at night with the fire and rain). Space replays, the arrow keys step through, Esc stops everything.

Optional upgrades
  • Real nature recordings for the biome ambience (forest birds by day, rainforest, alpine wind, a wolf howl). My synthesized ones work for now.
Most sounds are made in code (Python, at 44.1 kHz); the animals, the chant and the drunk hiker are Wyatt's Pixabay finds, trimmed and leveled. Everything is mastered to three loudness levels, plus a quieter one for loops. The game gets OGG files with MP3 as a fallback; this page plays the MP3s.
